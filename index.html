02 Available commands

One word for everything
you want to know.

Every command is sent as a normal Telegram message, from an authorized chat. The bot processes it, the app executes, and the answer goes back to the chat — with a heads-up when it matters.

/traccia

Location

One-shot high-accuracy GPS. Reply with a Telegram location card, accuracy and battery.

/foto [front|back]

Snapshot

Visible 5-second heads-up notification on the device, then a silent capture. Photo sent to the chat.

/audio [seconds]

Recording

Up to 30 seconds. Always preceded by a visible heads-up on the phone.

/stato

Diagnostics

Battery, signal, last known location, active modes. Without engaging any sensor.

/tutto

Full check

Location + photo + audio in sequence. For when you need to know everything at once.

/trovami · optional

Ring

Rings the phone at max volume, even if silenced. For locating it at home.

04 Transparency, always

You always see who asked
what, and when.

Every event is recorded in the audit log — visible on this phone, at any time, even to the person who uses the app. Requests, quiet-hour blocks, unauthorized attempts: all tracked, all readable.

  • i.

    Persistent notification

    Never a hidden background service. Family Tracker is overt: a fixed "listening to the family" notification is always visible.

  • ii.

    Heads-up before every action

    Photo and audio require a visible 5-second heads-up notification on the device. No surprises.

  • iii.

    Token in the Keystore

    The Telegram bot token lives in the hardware-backed Android Keystore. Never exfiltrated, never in the clear.

  • iv.

    Family whitelist

    Only approved chats can send commands. Everything else is silently recorded and ignored.
